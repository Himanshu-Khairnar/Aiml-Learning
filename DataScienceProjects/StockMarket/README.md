# Stock Market Analysis (Reliance Industries)

This project conducts a technical analysis of Reliance Industries stock data (post-2020) to identify trends, risks, and potential returns.

## Features

- **Trend Analysis**: Implementing Simple Moving Averages (20-day & 50-day) to identify "Golden Cross" buy signals.
- **Risk Analysis**: Analyzing volatility through daily returns calculation and histogram plotting.
- **Volume Analysis**: Tracking trading volume with a 15-day moving average.
- **Investment Simulation**: Simulating the returns of a ₹1 Lakh investment made at the beginning of the dataset period.

## Observations

- **Trend Identification**: The crossover of 20-day and 50-day moving averages (Golden Cross) helps in identifying potential bullish trends.
- **Volatility**: The histogram of daily returns typically shows a bell curve; a wider curve indicates higher volatility and risk associated with the stock.
- **Long-term Growth**: The simple investment simulation demonstrates the power of compounding and long-term holding strategies in the stock market.

## Technologies Used

- **Python**
- **Pandas**: For time-series data handling and analysis.
- **Matplotlib**: For visualizing stock trends and distributions.
- **NumPy**: For numerical calculations.

## Files

- `StockMarket.ipynb`: Analysis notebook.
- `RELIANCE.csv`: Historical stock data.
